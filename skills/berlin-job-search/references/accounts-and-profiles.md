# Accounts and profiles

> The user creates and controls every account. The agent may draft text and compare settings but never logs in, enters passwords/recovery codes/2FA, solves CAPTCHAs, downloads account data or changes visibility. Stop for login, consent or identity checks.

## LinkedIn

1. Join at [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) and complete identity and email/phone verification yourself; use a professional email you control.
2. Use a truthful headline: target role + specialty/evidence + city/work model. Patterns: `Senior Backend Engineer | Java, AWS, platform reliability | Berlin`; `Data Engineer | Python, SQL und Datenplattformen | Berlin`; or `Operations Analyst transitioning to Data Analytics | SQL, dashboards, process improvement`.
3. Write the About section in first person: target roles, one or two explainable outcomes, relevant tools, languages and location/relocation preferences. Do not add claims you cannot substantiate or publish work-authorization details unless you choose.
4. List roles in reverse chronological order with scope/outcomes; keep dates/facts consistent across English and German profile versions. See [add a profile language](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a541878).
5. Set target locations/roles and choose recruiter-only or public Open to Work visibility. The green frame may display publicly, and recruiter-only visibility is not guaranteed private. Check [current visibility guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a507508).
6. Order real skills by relevance; request endorsements only from people who can support them. Add projects only when you can explain your contribution. Use [LinkedIn job alerts](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a507441) for a small set of distinct role searches.
7. Treat Easy Apply as an application, not a quality signal. Verify the destination, CV, answers and consent. Connect with contextual notes; do not mass-send or invent a referral.

### Exporting profile information

The user can organize an export into the private local `job-search/cv.md`, then confirm every fact, date, role, skill and qualification. LinkedIn's **Save to PDF** may be unavailable to some users, requires English profile language and is desktop-only ([Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a541960)). To request a data archive, open **Me → Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → How LinkedIn uses your data → Download your data**, choose only needed categories, and request it; LinkedIn emails the link and the archive lasts 72 hours ([Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1339364)). Keep exports local, remove irrelevant connection/contact data, and do not upload them to an untrusted service.

### Alerts and application files

Use separate alerts for unrelated role families; recheck city, radius, freshness and workplace filters when the UI changes. LinkedIn may retain uploaded application CVs for reuse; choose and remove them yourself ([resume settings](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a510363)). Review search-engine visibility, contact details and old salary/location/permit answers. Verify employer/agency domains and privacy notices before uploading.

## XING

XING remains an active German-language jobs network, with job search, recruiter discovery, profile preferences and employer recruiting. New Work SE described the repositioning in 2025. Sources: [XING job-search guide](https://help.xing.com/hc/en-us/articles/31499161470237-Find-your-new-job-with-XING), [job preferences](https://help.xing.com/hc/en-us/articles/31499254751133-Modify-your-job-preferences), and [New Work SE strategy](https://new-work.se/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-petra-von-strombeck-hands-over-chairmanship-of-managing-board).

Create and verify the account yourself at [XING](https://www.xing.com/). Complete work history, skills and target roles; check visibility before adding contact details. Set job-seeker status, city, salary, level, hours, workplace model and industries. Save relevant alerts yourself, verify the employer's current vacancy, and use concise, contextual recruiter notes. XING says its job-preference settings can exclude the current employer; read the current privacy wording before relying on that setting.

## StepStone, Indeed and public services

- **StepStone:** use [StepStone Germany](https://www.stepstone.de/), review CV discoverability, set Jobagent filters, split distinct role families into separate alerts, and remove outdated CV files yourself.
- **Indeed:** use [Indeed Germany](https://de.indeed.com/); distinguish public profile visibility from a one-off CV upload, check whether a listing is direct or agency-posted, and verify redirects/privacy notices at the employer's site.
- **Agentur für Arbeit:** [Jobsuche](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/jobsuche/) works without an account. Create an account yourself only for eServices such as job-seeker registration; identity checks may apply. See [eServices](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/eservices).
- **Make it in Germany:** [job listings](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/working-in-germany/job-listings) and [visa information](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence) help international candidates, but do not replace the responsible mission/Ausländerbehörde. Its free [Working and Living in Germany hotline](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/advice-support/hotline) answers job-search, recognition and visa questions; see [visa and permits](visa-and-work-permits.md).
- **EURES:** [job search](https://eures.europa.eu/jobseekers_en) covers European cross-border listings and mobility support; verify payroll country, work location and permit conditions.
- **kununu / Glassdoor:** [kununu](https://www.kununu.com/) and [Glassdoor Germany](https://www.glassdoor.de/) provide employer, salary and interview context. Reviews are self-selected and may be stale; compare dates, roles and locations.
- For public-sector jobs, keep accurate German-language titles and qualifications available; postings may require formal recognition or other eligibility evidence.

## Privacy checklist

- Use a unique password, platform security protections and recovery methods you control.
- Review third-party app access, search-engine visibility, contact details, uploaded CVs and activity notifications; remove stale or confidential material.
- Do not share passwords, one-time codes, cookies, ID scans, residence documents, BundID passwords, eID PINs or recovery codes with agents or recruiters. Use official account-recovery channels.
- Store exports locally and privately. The user reviews and approves each profile edit, alert, application, message or upload; the agent never clicks Apply/Submit/Send.
- Do not publicly announce a pending employment end unless you choose to disclose it.
