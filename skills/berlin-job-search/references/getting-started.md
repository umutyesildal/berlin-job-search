# Getting started

> Practical triage, not a permit or benefit decision. Do not share passport/tax identifiers, account credentials or private documents unless needed for a specific local task.

## Intake and route

Ask about citizenship/current permit, current country, German/English level, target role/level, employment end date, city/radius, remote preference, salary floor and contract type. Use the answers to select the next guide:

| Situation | First step |
|---|---|
| Permit unclear, moving from abroad or non-EU qualification | Check [visa/work permits](visa-and-work-permits.md), recognition and the responsible authority before travel or work; the official Quick-Check, Opportunity Card Self-Check and free hotline are listed there. |
| Already resident and searching | Set a focused role list, select sources in [job sources](job-sources.md), and create accurate German/English profile/CV drafts. |
| German below B2 | Prefer ads explicitly accepting English or saying German is optional; inferred language remains uncertain. Compare real levels with the [glossary](glossary.md) and [analysis guide](api-sources.md). |
| Regulated job | Confirm recognition/license requirements with the competent authority before assuming equivalence. |
| Unemployed or job ending | Read [unemployment and support](unemployment-and-support.md); register by BA deadlines below. |
| First application or offer | Use [applications in Germany](applications-germany.md), then review terms with [interviews and offers](interviews-and-offers.md); compare gross compensation, not guessed net pay. |

| Work situation | Search implication and first check |
|---|---|
| EU/EEA/Swiss citizen | Usually free-movement rights; confirm country-specific registration obligations. [EU guidance](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/living-permanently/eu-citizens). |
| Third-country national in Germany | Search roles allowed by the residence card/Zusatzblatt; verify employer, job type, hours and start date. Ask the Ausländerbehörde before changing title/employer. |
| Third-country national abroad with an offer | A signed offer may support a work visa/Blue Card but does not authorize work. Confirm qualification, recognition/license, salary and responsible mission. [Visa types](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types). |
| Third-country national abroad without an offer | Compare remote applications with a qualifying job-search route; verify Opportunity Card eligibility, funds, language/qualification and mission process. [Opportunity Card](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card/job-search). |

## Employment deadlines

Register **arbeitsuchend** no later than three months before a known employment end, or within three days of learning of a sooner end. Register separately **arbeitslos** by the first day without work; the earlier registration does not replace it. Contact the [BA](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/ihre-schritte-wenn-sie-arbeitslos-werden/wie-sie-sich-arbeitsuchend-melden) promptly if a deadline may have passed; benefit eligibility is individual. An employee whose notice period has started should verify the contract end date and register on time; if the contract ends within three months, do not wait for the end date.

BA eServices may require an appointment or identity check; see the [eServices overview](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/eservices).

## First week after arrival

1. Arrange temporary housing and find a Bürgeramt appointment. Bring the requested identity/housing documents, including the landlord's **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung** if required; don't pay an unverified landlord/intermediary. The federal [Wohnsitz anmelden service](https://verwaltung.bund.de/leistungsverzeichnis/de/leistung/99115005104000) says registration is due within two weeks of moving in; availability varies by municipality.
2. Look for the Steuer-ID, normally mailed after registration. If it does not arrive, use [BZSt guidance](https://www.bzst.de/EN/Private_individuals/Tax_identification_number/tax_identification_number_node.html).
3. Ask the statutory pension insurer or employer about the Sozialversicherungsnummer if you have not worked in Germany. [Deutsche Rentenversicherung](https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/DRV/EN/Versicherung).
4. Arrange appropriate health insurance before work/study; compare statutory and private eligibility with an adviser. [Overview](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/living-in-germany/health-insurance).
5. Open a bank account if useful for salary/rent/bills; compare fees and local requirements. No provider is endorsed.
6. Keep official notices/deadlines. Provide tax/insurance data only to a verified employer via secure HR, never to a recruiter.

## Search plan

- Choose one or two target titles, seniority, primary city and one or two alternatives; see [cities and regions](cities-and-regions.md).
- Set acceptable radius, remote/on-site policy, contract type and annual gross salary using [salary and compensation](salary-and-compensation.md).
- Record permit limits privately; don't infer work authorization from nationality or an ad.
- Choose language-appropriate CV/letter versions from `assets/`, and set a weekly search/application/follow-up schedule.
- Keep a private tracker; review every listing before saving or contacting.
- For a first German application, use [applications in Germany](applications-germany.md) and the templates in `assets/`; for an offer, review [interviews and offers](interviews-and-offers.md).

For the first 30 days: days 1–2 triage permit/city/dealbreakers; days 3–5 prepare truthful CV versions; week 2 search focused role families and verify employer pages; week 3 prepare interviews and individualized outreach; week 4 review responses and adjust titles, locations or language filters. This is a planning cadence, not a deadline.

## Moving from abroad

Before booking travel, decide whether to apply remotely, use an eligible job-search route or secure an offer first. Separate applications possible from abroad from tasks requiring a German address; never claim a German location you do not have. Check recognition, real language levels, visa/insurance/housing/deposit/travel costs, worksite/remote terms and the responsible mission. Keep identity documents out of routine recruiter outreach and identify a fallback before permit deadlines. The [Opportunity Card](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card/job-search) is not a default route; verify eligibility and mission instructions.

## Weekly routine

| Block | Work | Stop when |
|---|---|---|
| Search | Narrow role/city/freshness query. | Login, CAPTCHA or an untrusted download is required. |
| Review | Read plausible title/location matches. | Employer or URL cannot be verified. |
| Employer check | Confirm the opening is live on the employer page. | Posting is closed or identity differs. |
| Application | Tailor one accurate CV/letter. | Facts or attachments need user confirmation. |
| Follow-up | Update the private tracker and due actions. | Never send a message automatically. |

For the wider source list, see [job sources](job-sources.md); Berlin-specific context is in [cities and regions](cities-and-regions.md). Protect time for language learning and permit/recognition paperwork; the search is not the only task in a move.
