# Visa and work permits

> Not legal advice. Rules depend on nationality, permit wording, qualifications, occupation and salary. Confirm with the responsible Ausländerbehörde or German mission before accepting work or travelling.

## Official self-checks and free advice

Make it in Germany is the federal government's portal for skilled workers moving to Germany. Point users to these tools; they run them themselves, and the results are orientation, not a decision:

- [Quick-Check](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-recognition/quick-check): asks what the user wants to do in Germany (work, study, vocational training or start a business) and links the matching visa pages.
- [Self-Check: Opportunity Card](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-recognition/opportunity-card/self-check): a non-binding first assessment of Chancenkarte eligibility, starting with nationality.
- [Working and Living in Germany hotline](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/advice-support/hotline): **+49 30 1815-1111**, Monday–Thursday 09:00–16:00 and Friday 09:00–12:00 CET, in German and English. Run by BAMF and the BA; advice on job search, recognition, visa and residence, and learning German is free, though call charges apply. The user calls; never call or write to it on their behalf.
- [Professions in demand](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/working-in-germany/job-search-application/professions-in-demand): sector overviews such as IT, engineering, nursing, medicine, skilled crafts, transport and science. Demand is not a permit; the lower Blue Card threshold applies only to occupations on the legal shortage list.

Make it in Germany does not place jobs. Its [job-search FAQ](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/working-in-germany/job-search-application) says recruiters or agencies offering paid services under its name are not part of the official portal; treat fee requests made in its name as a red flag. Checked 2026-11-01.

## 2026 salary and route snapshot

Amounts are gross annual salary unless marked otherwise. Thresholds can change each year; salary alone does not qualify someone for a permit.

| Route / rule | 2026 requirement |
|---|---|
| EU Blue Card, regular | €50,700. |
| Blue Card, shortage occupation or recent graduate | €45,934.20 for shortage occupations or graduates who completed a degree within the last three years; BA approval applies to this lower-threshold category. |
| First skilled-worker title, age 45+ | €55,770 or evidence of adequate pension provision. |
| §6 BeschV IT specialist without formal qualification | Two years of relevant experience in the last five years, a qualified IT job and €45,630 salary; the threshold exception applies to employers bound by a collective agreement that pays its applicable terms. BA approval applies. |
| Chancenkarte living funds | €1,091 net/month; prove with a blocked account, declaration of commitment or qualifying income. |

Sources: [BA/ZAV Blue Card (2026)](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/zav/working-and-living-in-germany/iss-en/issue-03-2026/blue-card), [Make it in Germany: Skilled Immigration Act](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-recognition/skilled-immigration-act/print), [BA/ZAV skilled workers](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/zav/working-and-living-in-germany/iss-en/issue-05-2026/alm-specialists), [§6 BeschV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/beschv_2013/__6.html), [German mission IT specialist information](https://rabat.diplo.de/ma-de/service/2704652-2704652), and [Chancenkarte job-search route](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card/job-search).

The §6 salary value is 45% of the annual pension-insurance assessment ceiling; the collective-agreement exception is in §6(1) BeschV. Confirm current rules with the relevant mission and BA/ZAV, not search snippets.

## Common routes

- **Skilled workers (§18a / §18b AufenthG):** §18a covers vocational training and §18b an academic qualification. Both generally require a qualified offer and recognized qualification; BA approval depends on the case. See [BA/ZAV specialists](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/zav/working-and-living-in-germany/iss-en/issue-05-2026/alm-specialists) and [Make it in Germany: work visa for qualified professionals](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/work-qualified-professionals).
- **EU/EEA/Swiss citizens:** generally no German work visa; registration and other obligations may apply. [Official overview](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/living-permanently/eu-citizens).
- **Recognition:** [Anerkennung in Deutschland](https://www.anerkennung-in-deutschland.de/html/en/index.php) locates the competent authority; [anabin](https://anabin.kmk.org/) lists institution/degree status; [ZAB Statement of Comparability](https://zab.kmk.org/en/statement-comparability) may be appropriate. Regulated professions can require a license and language proof.
- **Students and graduates:** see the dedicated section below for student work permission, social insurance and the post-study job-search route.
- **§81a fast track:** an employer can request the accelerated skilled-worker procedure with the worker's authorization and a fee. It coordinates steps but does not guarantee a visa. [Official procedure](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/skilled-immigration-act/accelerated-procedure).
- **Berlin LEA:** the [Business Immigration Service](https://www.berlin.de/einwanderung/service/business-immigration-service/) supports employers with skilled-worker residence/work matters; appointments and cases covered can change. [LEA contact](https://www.berlin.de/einwanderung/en/about-us/contact/artikel.1500715.en.php).

## Students and graduates

Figures and rules below are the 2026 snapshot, accessed 24 October 2026. The permit card and *Zusatzblatt* control the individual case; confirm with the local Ausländerbehörde before starting or changing work.

|---|---|---|

Student auxiliary work at a higher-education institution is not counted. | [Residence Act §16b(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__16b.html); [Make it in Germany — working during studies](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/study-vocational-training/studies-in-germany/work) (2026 guidance, accessed 2026-10-24). |

Do not infer net pay from gross salary or student status alone. | [Federal Ministry of Finance wage-tax calculator](https://www.bmf-steuerrechner.de/), accessed 2026-10-24. |

The residence-permit workday account and the social-insurance 20-hour test are different checks. A working-student contract or a LinkedIn “Full-time” label does not resolve either one; check the exact hours, title, permit wording, enrollment and other jobs with the responsible authority/insurer.

## Chancenkarte

The Opportunity Card allows eligible non-EU/EFTA nationals to seek work for up to one year. Recognized skilled workers use the no-points route; the points route requires six points and a qualifying qualification, plus A1 German or B2 English. Holders may work up to 20 hours/week and trial work for up to two weeks per employer. Financial proof is €1,091 net/month in 2026. See [job-search route](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card/job-search) and [questions and answers](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card/questions-answers).

From 1 October 2026, some missions, including the German mission in the UK, require applications through the [Consular Services Portal](https://digital.diplo.de/chancenkarte). This is mission-specific; follow the [UK mission's instructions](https://uk.diplo.de/uk-en/02/visa/chancenkarte-2659588). Budget for the full stay, insurance, rent/deposit, travel and recognition costs.

## Check before searching, travelling or accepting

- Read the residence card and Zusatzblatt: permitted work type, hours and employer restrictions. If unclear, ask the Ausländerbehörde.
- Check whether the role is qualified or regulated, whether the degree is recognized, and whether a license or language requirement applies.
- Verify guaranteed annual gross salary, actual hours and which pay components count. A bonus or speculative equity is not automatically qualifying salary.
- Confirm whether BA approval, a recognition decision or §81a fast track is needed.
- Identify the responsible mission or Ausländerbehörde and its current portal, appointment and document process. The [Federal Foreign Office visa overview](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/visa-service) and [Make it in Germany visa overview](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence) are starting points.
- If permit conditions or process change, request written guidance from the competent mission/Ausländerbehörde.
- Prepare only documents the authority requests: passport/current permit; signed offer/contract with salary, hours, title, worksite and start date; degree, training and transcript; required recognition, certified copies/translations; employer declaration (**Erklärung zum Beschäftigungsverhältnis**); insurance/funds; recent experience evidence; pension evidence if the age rule applies; and any professional license.
- Confirm the legal employer, worksite and role match the proposed route. A job offer is not a permit. Do not send identity documents to an unsolicited recruiter or pay an unverified visa intermediary.

## Qualification recognition

1. Identify whether the occupation is regulated and which German authority decides recognition.
2. Check institution and qualification status in [anabin](https://anabin.kmk.org/); a database result is not always a formal recognition decision.
3. If needed, request a [ZAB Statement of Comparability](https://zab.kmk.org/en/statement-comparability) or locate the competent body through [Anerkennung in Deutschland](https://www.anerkennung-in-deutschland.de/html/en/index.php).
4. For regulated work, verify licensing, language, supervised practice and adaptation requirements.
5. Keep awards, transcripts, required translations and authority correspondence together. Academic comparability is not a professional license.

## Blue Card employer changes

Ask the Ausländerbehörde before resigning or starting a new role. Confirm whether the new job remains eligible, whether the authority needs notice/approval during the initial period, whether salary remains above the current threshold, whether the employer must update its declaration, and whether family permits are affected. The answer depends on the permit issue date and current law. **(verify)** See [Make it in Germany: EU Blue Card](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/eu-blue-card).
