Startup listings are leads: check runway, product, reporting line and office attendance on the employer site. Public-sector roles may specify citizenship/security conditions; healthcare and Ausbildung may require German, licensing or recognition. Check student work limits before accepting student/intern hours; see [visa and permits](visa-and-work-permits.md). Agencies may be the legal employer; verify client, assignment duration, pay and collective terms. Before freelance work, check worker classification, tax, insurance and permit conditions. Remote roles may restrict country, payroll and time zone. Use [kununu](https://www.kununu.com/) and [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.de/) for employer/salary/interview research; reviews are self-selected and may be stale. Use the [BA Entgeltatlas](https://web.arbeitsagentur.de/entgeltatlas/) for pay distributions and [Berlin Partner](https://www.berlin-partner.de/en/) for directories.

## Find unlisted ATS boards

Search engines can expose public employer boards:

```text
site:jobs.ashbyhq.com Berlin software engineer
site:job-boards.greenhouse.io Berlin data analyst
site:jobs.lever.co Berlin product manager
site:jobs.personio.de Berlin customer success
site:join.com/companies Berlin engineer
site:*.recruitee.com Berlin operations
```

Hosts and indexing change; confirm on the employer career page. To add a company to `assets/companies.json`, identify its ATS and slug from its career URL, use the matching endpoint in [API sources](api-sources.md), and verify the public feed returns at least one current job. Use an exact supported `ats` value; never guess a slug.
