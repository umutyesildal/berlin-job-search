# Privacy policy

Berlin Job Search is an open-source skill and set of local scripts. The publisher runs no server
for it and collects no user data. There is no analytics or tracking.

## Data and purpose

You may share a CV, profile, job preferences, or application details with the AI assistant you use
(such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Codex) to search for jobs, assess postings, draft documents, or track
your search. That provider processes the information under its own privacy policy. When the scripts
run locally, personal files are stored on your machine in `job-search/`.

The scripts send search terms, city, filters, and job URLs to the relevant public job sources:
Bundesagentur für Arbeit, Arbeitnow, Daily Berlin Jobs, and company career boards such as
Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Personio, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee, and Workable. They do not send your
CV or profile to those sources.

## Recipients and retention

The publisher receives and retains nothing. AI providers and job sources process requests under
their own policies. Local files remain on your machine until you delete them.

## Your controls

Delete `job-search/` to remove local files, and uninstall the plugin to stop using it. For questions,
open an issue at <https://github.com/umutyesildal/berlin-job-search/issues>.

Berlin Job Search does not ask for passwords, government ID numbers, or bank details.
